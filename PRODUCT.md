# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Back-office clerks and operations staff at Italian pension fund administrators (fondi pensione). They process Ercole module submissions sent by clients: adhesion forms, beneficiary designations, transfers, redemptions. Users are in their 30s-50s, expert in the Ercole module vocabulary and pension procedures, non-technical. They work on desktop or laptop in a standard office environment during business hours, processing 50-200 documents per day.

Secondary users: financial advisors submitting documents on behalf of clients, and supervisors reviewing processed batches.

## Product Purpose

DocumentAI automates the classification and data extraction from multi-page Ercole pension fund documents. Instead of manually reading each page and categorizing requests, the system runs an AI pipeline that: identifies which Ercole modules are present (40+ module types), extracts contact and form data, assigns pages to each request, and returns a structured summary. The operator reviews the AI output, optionally asks follow-up questions in natural language, and passes the structured result to downstream systems.

Success looks like: an operator can review a 10-page multi-request document in under 2 minutes instead of 15.

## Brand Personality

Precise, institutional, trustworthy. The product must feel like an instrument built by people who understand the domain, not a generic AI tool. Warm but not casual. The voice of a knowledgeable colleague who has read every Ercole specification.

Three words: precise, grounded, authoritative.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards: Notion, Airtable, Monday.com templates (icon grids, card layouts, pastel palette)
- Consumer chatbot interfaces: ChatGPT, Gemini (centered chat bubbles, rounded pill inputs)
- Generic blue financial software: banking portals, legacy Italian government systems (navy + white, flat tables, no hierarchy)
- Over-designed FinTech: crypto dashboards, neon accents, glassmorphism, dark-mode-by-default
- Document processing UIs that feel like scanning apps (too utilitarian, no editorial quality)

## Design Principles

1. Results before explanation: The extracted data is the product. Show it immediately, expose reasoning on demand.
2. One task, one moment: The interface supports a single focused action per session. No competing panels, no dashboard sprawl.
3. Trust through precision: Confidence scores and verifiable data are first-class. The AI should never hide its uncertainty.
4. The tool disappears: When it works well, the operator sees the document result, not the interface.
5. Italian institutional gravity: Warm, precise, legible. A quality tool from a quality house. Not cold government bureaucracy and not flashy consumer SaaS.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Italian-language content primary (UI labels in Italian). Left-to-right text. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. High contrast for extended office use. No color-only information.
